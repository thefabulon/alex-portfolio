How the race works
  • Three laps around the cone track, against the other four characters.
  • When you're not racing, the other four still drive laps around the track at their own easy pace, so you've always got someone to chase. Once they finish a race, they keep cruising too.
  • Stay between the cones. Cutting across the middle doesn't count toward your lap.
  • Enter starts a race. R puts you back on the track.
VR controls
  • Right trigger: gas. Left trigger: brake / reverse.
  • Either thumbstick: steer (left stick up/down also drives).
  • Hold both grip buttons and turn your hands like a real wheel to steer.
  • A: switch between sitting in the kart and standing beside it (RC mode).
  • B: recenter your view. Y: put the kart back on the track. X: start a race.
