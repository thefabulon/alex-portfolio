How to drift
  • Steer into a corner and hold Space to kick the back end out.
  • Keep holding it, with the gas down, to hold the slide. Steer in harder to tighten it.
  • Let go of Space to straighten up. Let go and press it again while you're counter-steering to flick into the next drift.
  • Longer drifts fill the meter and build a multiplier, up to ×5. Brushing a wall is fine, but hitting one hard loses the combo.

On a phone or tablet: arrows on the left to steer, gas and brake pedals on the right, and the pink DRIFT button for the handbrake.

VR controls
  • Right trigger: gas. Left trigger: brake / reverse.
  • Either thumbstick: steer. Click and hold either thumbstick in: handbrake (let go to straighten up).
  • Hold both grip buttons and turn your hands like a real wheel to steer.
  • A: switch between sitting in the kart and watching from the stands. The sign about the kart is straight across the track from the stands.
  • B: recenter your view. Y: put the kart back on the track. X: start a race.
How the race works
  • Two laps of the drift track against the other four characters.
  • When you're not racing, the other four still drive laps around the track at their own easy pace, so you've always got someone to chase. Once they finish a race, they keep cruising too.
  • You get drift points during the race as well. They show up in your results.
About the kart

    Brian's Skyline is on show in the middle of the track, turning on a stand, so you can see the kart and the car it came from together. Try it at night to see the underglow.
