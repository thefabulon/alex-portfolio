How to wheelie
  • Get rolling, then hold Space to lift the front. Low gears lift it faster.
  • Let go of Space to bring it down. Tap it to hold the angle.
  • Past the orange mark on the meter, your hand drags on the road and sparks fly. Past the red line, you loop it.
Jumps
  • Ride over a crest, off a ridge or off the top of a slope fast enough and you get air.
  • In the air, hold Space to pull the nose up and the brake to push it down.
  • Land on the back wheel and you ride away in a wheelie. Land nose first, or too hard, and you crash. Landing on a downslope is softest.

On a phone or tablet: steer with the arrows on the left; brake, wheelie and gas on the right.

Gauges
  • The bar across the top is the rev counter: white, then green when it's time to shift, then red at the limit.
  • The dash shows your speed in mph, the gear and the RPM. The bicycles show how fast your legs turn and which gear teeth you're in. The Sur-Ron is electric, so it's always in D.
  • The sign next to the minimap is the real speed limit. Your speed turns red when you're 10 mph over.
About the map

The roads, buildings, coastline and city lines are real, from OpenStreetMap, and the hills come from real elevation data. It covers Laguna Hills and the 5 and 405 down to Dana Point, and Laguna Beach across to Mission Viejo.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (USGS, SRTM).
