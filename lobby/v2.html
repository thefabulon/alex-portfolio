The Lobby

All of Alexander's projects, one game cartridge each. Take a cartridge from the rack, plug it into the console, then step through the portal.

Drag to look around. Walk with WASD, or click the floor. Click things to pick them up and use them. Esc puts a cartridge back.

Cartridges
In a headset

Trigger or grip grabs a cartridge. Let go over the console to plug it in. Left stick walks, right stick turns, and pointing at the floor and pulling the trigger jumps you there.
