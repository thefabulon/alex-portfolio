What's in the gallery

1The Lobby

A 70s den where every game is a cartridge, and a little robot named Bit shows you around.

Open the lobby →

What's there

An 8 × 6 m den. A wood cabinet holds the CRT TV, the wood-grain ALEX-2600 console, a joystick and a six-slot cartridge rack. Round rug, synthwave window, posters, a beanbag and a plant.

How you move

Start on the rug facing the cabinet. Take a cartridge from the rack (1), plug it into the console (2), and the portal by the window opens (3). Walk through it into the game. Bit flies between four stops and points at whatever it's talking about.

Why six slots

There are only three games, so the empty slots hold "Coming soon" cartridges. A new game drops into a slot without changing the room.

2Go-Kart

My plywood kart on a walled drift track, with Brian's Skyline turning on a stand in the middle.

Open the go-kart →

What's there

A closed track with concrete barriers both sides, red-and-white curbs on the bends, a start gantry, a grandstand, nine light towers and hills far off.

How you move

Start at the back of a five-kart grid. Race the crew for two laps, or drive free while they cruise laps on their own. Hold Space to drift.

Design choice

The Skyline goes wherever the infield has the most room, worked out from the track's shape, so it stays in the middle if the track changes. The sign about how I built the kart faces the grandstand, so the Stands camera looks right at it.

Traced from gokart/v2.html · built, v2

3Wheelie Sim

One straight road that never ends. Pull the front up and see how far back you dare to go.

Open the wheelie sim →

What's there

An 11 m two-lane road with white edge lines, pine trees and Alpinestars signs on both sides. Five rides: street, trail and sprint bikes, a Sur-Ron and a Kawasaki Ninja H2R.

How you move

Speed up and hold Space to lift the front. Past about 66° your rider drags a hand on the road. Past about 85° you loop out and crash. C switches between 3rd person, 1st person and side cam.

Design choice

The bike never really moves forward. The road markings, trees and signs slide toward the camera, and once they pass behind you they jump back ahead, like a scrolling backdrop on a stage.

Traced from wheelie-ride/src/main.js · built, v0.11

4Drift Garage

Build a car in the garage, then take it to one of three courses.

Open the drift garage →

What's there

A build bay with five rides (AE86, Silvia S15, Supra MK4, RX-7 FD, Ninja H2) and tuning: paint, camber, wheel offset, spoiler, wide-body, turbo and sponsor stickers. Then three courses.

How you move

Tune your build and save it, pick a course, then drift for points and combos. On the Playground you weave through the cones, and they go flying if you hit them.

Design choice

Each course has its own scenery so it feels like a different place: containers, cranes and water at the dockyard, trees, rocks and guardrails on the mountain road, and an open lot with cones and light poles at the playground.
