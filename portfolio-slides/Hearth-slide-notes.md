## Slide 3: Colors that match your mood

Rubric: Creative Expression (themes drawn from personal memories, a unique perspective), Creativity Ability (exploring four directions instead of one), Artistic Ability / Craftsmanship (consistent, readable palettes), Communication (the why behind each color).
Stories: Ember is the campfire on camping trips with Alex's mom, and the reason the app is called Hearth. Tidepool is exploring tide pools as a kid, where the ecosystem works as one, a picture of serenity and unity. Morning Meadow is waking up hopeful, lying in the grass under a blue sky with nowhere to be. Marshy is his childhood stuffed sheep in midnight blue and sky blue sequins, who comforted him on the darkest nights and starred in a cartoon he made as a kid.
Color talking points: blue is widely linked with calm; green with nature, growth and balance. Every theme is muted, avoids pure black and white, keeps text easy to read, and keeps one warm note so it still feels like a hearth.
Interview: answers 'What is your personal favorite design aesthetic?'
