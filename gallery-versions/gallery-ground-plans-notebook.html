1

The Lobby

open it →

A 70s den where every game is a cartridge, and a little robot named Bit shows you around.

  • 8 × 6 m den. A wood cabinet holds the CRT TV, the wood-grain ALEX-2600 console, a joystick and a 6-slot cartridge rack
  • round rug, synthwave window, posters, a beanbag and a plant
  • you start on the rug facing the cabinet. Take a cartridge from the rack (1), plug it into the console (2), and the portal by the window opens (3). Walk through it into the game
  • Bit flies between 4 stops and points at whatever it's talking about
  • 6 slots but only 2 games, so the empty slots hold "coming soon" cartridges. A new game drops into a slot without changing the room

traced from lobby/v2.html · built, v2.11

2

Go-Kart

open it →

My plywood kart on a walled drift track, with Brian's Skyline turning on a stand in the middle.

  • a closed track with concrete barriers both sides, red-and-white curbs on the bends, a start gantry, a grandstand, 9 light towers and hills far off
  • start at the back of a 5-kart grid. Race the crew for 2 laps, or drive free while they cruise laps on their own. Hold Space to drift
  • the Skyline goes wherever the infield has the most room, worked out from the track's shape, so it stays in the middle if the track changes
  • the sign about how I built the kart faces the grandstand across the front straight, so the Stands camera looks right at it

traced from gokart/v2.html · built, v2

3

Wheelie Sim

open it →

One straight road that never ends. Pull the front up and see how far back you dare to go.

  • an 11 m two-lane road with white edge lines, pine trees and Alpinestars signs on both sides
  • 5 rides: street, trail and sprint bikes, a Sur-Ron and a Kawasaki Ninja H2R
  • speed up and hold Space to lift the front. Past about 66° your rider drags a hand on the road. Past about 85° you loop out and crash
  • C switches between 3rd person, 1st person and side cam
  • the bike never really moves forward. The road, trees and signs slide toward the camera, and once they pass behind you they jump back ahead, like a scrolling backdrop on a stage

traced from wheelie-ride/src/main.js · built, v0.11

Where it started:
10 early ideas

Before the games, I sketched ten ways to build a 3D gallery you could walk or drive through. Star the ones worth keeping.

How to pick one

Designers narrow down by testing each idea against a few questions. Score the starred ones 1 to 3 on each:

  1. Does it show my process? Is there room for sketches, early versions and mistakes, or only finished photos?
  2. Does it tell a story? Would a visitor feel a beginning, middle and end?
  3. Does it feel like me? Could only Alex have designed this space?
  4. Can I build it in time? A simple space done well beats a big one left half finished.

You can also combine plans. For example, enter through The Shop, then walk into the Black Box for the reveal.
