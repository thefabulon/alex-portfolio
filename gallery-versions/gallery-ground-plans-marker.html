The Lobby

A 70s den where every game is a cartridge, and a little robot named Bit shows you around.

Open it →

What's there

An 8 × 6 m den. A wood cabinet holds the CRT TV, the wood-grain ALEX-2600 console, a joystick and a 6-slot cartridge rack.

Round rug, synthwave window, posters, a beanbag and a plant.

How you move

Start on the rug facing the cabinet. Take a cartridge from the rack (1), plug it into the console (2), and the portal by the window opens (3). Walk through it into the game.

Bit flies between 4 stops and points at whatever it's talking about.

Why 6 slots?

The rack has 6 slots but there are only 2 games, so the empty slots hold "Coming soon" cartridges. A new game drops into a slot without changing the room.

Go-Kart

My plywood kart on a walled drift track, with Brian's Skyline turning on a stand in the middle.

Open it →

What's there

A closed track with concrete barriers both sides, red-and-white curbs on the bends, a start gantry, a grandstand, 9 light towers and hills far off.

How you move

Start at the back of a 5-kart grid. Race the crew for 2 laps, or drive free while they cruise laps on their own. Hold Space to drift.

The Skyline sits wherever the infield has the most room, so it stays in the middle if the track changes. The build sign faces the grandstand, so the Stands camera looks right at it.

Traced from gokart/v2.html · built, v2

Wheelie Sim

One straight road that never ends. Pull the front up and see how far back you dare to go.

Open it →

What's there

An 11 m two-lane road, pine trees and Alpinestars signs both sides. 5 rides: street, trail and sprint bikes, a Sur-Ron and a Kawasaki Ninja H2R.

How it works

Hold Space to lift the front. Past about 66° your rider drags a hand. Past about 85° you loop out. C switches cameras.

The bike never really moves. The road, trees and signs slide toward you and jump back ahead once they're behind, like a scrolling backdrop on a stage.
