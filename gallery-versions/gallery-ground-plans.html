The lobby and the two games

You start in the lobby. Every game is a cartridge: plug one into the console and a portal opens into that game.

Front door

The Lobby

Open it →

A 70s den where every game is a cartridge, and a little robot named Bit shows you around.

What's thereAn 8 × 6 m den. A wood cabinet holds the CRT TV, the wood-grain ALEX-2600 console, a joystick and a six-slot cartridge rack. Round rug, synthwave window, posters, a beanbag and a plant.
How you moveYou start on the rug facing the cabinet. Take a cartridge from the rack (1), plug it into the console (2), and the portal by the window opens (3). Walk through it into the game. Bit flies between four stops and points at whatever it's talking about.
Design choiceThe rack has six slots but there are only two games, so the empty slots hold "Coming soon" cartridges. A new game drops into a slot without changing the room.

Game 1

Go-Kart

Open it →

My plywood kart on a walled drift track, with Brian's Skyline turning on a stand in the middle.

What's thereA closed track with concrete barriers both sides, red-and-white curbs on the bends, a start gantry, a grandstand, nine light towers and hills far off.
How you moveStart at the back of a five-kart grid. Race the crew for two laps, or drive free while they cruise laps on their own. Hold Space to drift.
Design choiceThe Skyline goes wherever the infield has the most room, worked out from the track's shape, so it stays in the middle if the track changes. The sign about how I built the kart faces the grandstand across the front straight, so the Stands camera looks right at it.

Game 2

Wheelie Sim

Open it →

One straight road that never ends. Pull the front up and see how far back you dare to go.

What's thereAn 11 m two-lane road with white edge lines, pine trees and alpinestars signs on both sides. Five rides: street, trail and sprint bikes, a Sur-Ron and a Kawasaki Ninja H2R.
How you moveSpeed up and hold Space to lift the front. Past about 66° your rider drags a hand on the road. Past about 85° you loop out and crash. C switches between 3rd person, 1st person and side cam.
Design choiceThe bike never really moves forward. The road markings, trees and signs slide toward the camera, and once they pass behind you they jump back ahead and come round again, like a scrolling backdrop on a stage.

Where it started: 10 early concepts

Before the games, I sketched ten ways to build a 3D gallery you could walk or drive through. Star the ones worth keeping.

How to pick one

Designers narrow down by testing each idea against a few questions. Score your starred plans 1 to 3 on each.

  1. Does it show my process? Is there room for sketches, early versions and mistakes, or only finished photos?
  2. Does it tell a story? Would a visitor feel a beginning, middle and end?
  3. Does it feel like me? Could only Alex have designed this space?
  4. Can I build it in time? A simple space done well beats a big one left half finished.

You can also combine plans. For example, enter through The Shop, then walk into the Black Box for the reveal.
